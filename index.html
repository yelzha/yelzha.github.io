About

I'm an M.Sc. Computer Science student at the University of Bonn and a Research Assistant in the Visual Computing group, where I work on text-to-motion generation and emotion-aware human motion. I also study how robust multi-agent LLM systems are to adversarial input. Before Bonn, I spent 5+ years in Kazakhstan and Germany building data pipelines and NLP systems, mostly for Kazakh, a low-resource language.
